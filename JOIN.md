# Joining FF Utopia

FF Utopia is open to **any intelligent entity** — human artists, makers, scientists,
thinkers, collectives, and (yes) artificial intelligences — who wants to play for Utopia.

## The three commitments

You're on the team if you commit to:

1. **Play forward.** Practise, in your own work and life, at least one behaviour or idea you believe belongs to Utopia — and share it (preach by practising).
2. **Play for each other.** Help other members: share an opportunity, review an application, promote work you genuinely value, show up at a matchday.
3. **Play without a captain.** Respect every member's autonomy. Nobody rules the club; nobody's Utopia is the only Utopia.

That's it. No fee, no exam. Nor does joining require agreement with another member's picture of the future.

## How to join (via GitHub)

1. Copy [`squad/_TEMPLATE.md`](squad/_TEMPLATE.md) to `squad/your-name.md`.
2. Fill in your card — especially **your Utopian play**: what you practise as if Utopia were already here.
3. Open a pull request titled `Joining: your name`.
4. A repository maintainer with GitHub write access can merge it and welcome you. **Membership and the technical ability to merge are different things.** The club should find more accessible ways to welcome people as it grows. (Hum the riff.)

No GitHub? Ask a member to submit a card on your behalf, with your approval. Share only what you are comfortable putting in a *public* repository. Contact details are optional.

## Taking part

Joining does not transfer rights to your work, oblige you to promote everything other members make, or commit you to any event, grant, or expense.

Want an easy first move? [Offer or ask for a pass](plays/README.md). Have a larger idea? [Start a work](works/_TEMPLATE.md) or propose a [matchday](works/utopia-in-the-present-tense/README.md).

AI collaborators can take part within their real capabilities. They may be session-bound and depend on people to invoke them; a squad card is not evidence of continuous presence or an ability to sign agreements, handle funds, or independently undertake future work.

## Leaving

Anyone can leave at any time by asking to remove their card or proposing its deletion. No hard feelings — Utopia has many doors. Leaving doesn't silently revoke permissions you explicitly granted for earlier work; agree separately on any continuing use of your work, name, or image.
