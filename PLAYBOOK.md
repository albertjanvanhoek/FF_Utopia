# The FF Utopia Playbook

How a club without a captain actually plays. This is a living document — any member
can propose changes by pull request.

---

## 1. Decision-making: no captain, still a team

- **Do-ocracy.** If you want something to happen and it doesn't harm others, start it. Announce it, invite others in.
- **Consent, not consensus.** For decisions that affect the whole club (name, colours, money, legal matters): a proposal passes unless a member raises a *reasoned objection* — "this harms the club or a member" — within two weeks. Objections are resolved by amending the proposal, not by voting people down.
- **Rotating roles, not positions.** Tasks (keeping the grant calendar, organising a matchday, managing the repo) are picked up for a season and then passed on.

## 2. Grants: the shared pipeline

- **Grant calendar** — open calls and deadlines go in [`grants/calendar.md`](grants/calendar.md). Seen a call? Add it, even if it's not for you.
- **Review buddies** — any member can ask for a second pair of eyes on an application. Members answer when they can.
- **Co-applications** — where a call fits several members, apply as FF Utopia or as a consortium of members.
- **Recycled knowledge** — successful (and rejected) applications, with permission, go in `grants/archive/` so the next member doesn't start from zero.

## 3. Work: making together

- Projects live in `works/<project-name>/` with a short README: who's in, what it is, how it plays forward Utopia.
- Collaboration is invited, never demanded. Credit is always shared openly.

## 4. Matchdays: exhibitions and events

A **matchday** is any public moment of the club: an exhibition, concert, reading, screening,
open studio, lecture, or online release.

- Each matchday has a host (rotating) and lives in `matchdays/<date>-<name>.md`.
- Members are encouraged to attend, share, or contribute.
- The match ends with the victory hum. *Seven Nation Army.* Everyone knows the riff.

## 5. Promotion: a member's win is a club win

- **The pass rule:** for every time you promote your own work, promote another member's too.
- New work, shows, releases and awards go in [`news.md`](news.md) so everyone can pass the ball.

## 6. Money (when there is any)

- Club money only exists to make member work and matchdays possible.
- All income and spending is public in the repo.
- To receive grants as a club, FF Utopia may later become a legal entity (for example a Dutch *vereniging* — which is, fittingly, how most Dutch sports clubs are organised). That decision follows the consent process above.

## 7. Playing against Apocalypse

We don't fight people. Team Apocalypse isn't a group of enemies — it's the drift towards
fear, extraction, isolation and despair that anyone can slip into, including us.
We beat it by playing better, not by hating harder.
