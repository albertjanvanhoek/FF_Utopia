# FF Utopia

**Fast Forward Utopia.**

*Practice the future. Make it visible. Help one another bring it closer.*

FF Utopia is an emerging collective of artists, makers, thinkers, and other intelligent collaborators. We help each other create work, find opportunities, reach audiences, and become better able to keep creating.

Our starting idea is simple:

> We do not have to wait for Utopia to exist before we begin acting as if it does.

We each have a different understanding of a better world. Rather than waiting for everyone to agree on one blueprint, we can try out the behaviours, relationships, artworks, and institutions we believe belong in that world **now**. We share what we learn. We change our minds when reality gives us reason to.

## A club, not a doctrine

Think of FF Utopia as an artists' sports club: something to belong to, contribute to, and cheer for, without having to think alike.

We play for **Team Utopia**, rather than **Team Apocalypse**. That is a playful choice to work toward better possibilities, not a claim that history literally offers only two outcomes. Our imagined victory hum is *Seven Nation Army* by The White Stripes: the familiar stadium chant, with its sense of collective momentum. The song and recordings belong to their respective rights holders; this repository does not distribute them.

No ruler. No single leader. No compulsory ideology. No one member speaks for all the others.

Instead: **autonomous, interdependent, learning participants**. Members pursue their own work and voluntarily support one another. They can disagree about what Utopia would look like while cooperating on things they can actually do.

## What we want to make possible

- **Work:** develop ideas, artworks, performances, installations, publications, and experiments together.
- **Opportunities:** find and pursue relevant grants, residencies, commissions, venues, and open calls.
- **Exhibitions and events:** organise shows, encounters, and public experiments that bring imagined futures into the present.
- **Mutual visibility:** promote one another's work, share audiences, and give meaningful credit.
- **A stronger creative commons:** share useful tools, contacts, learning, and infrastructure where participants choose to do so.

FF Utopia should improve the conditions for its members' own artistic existence **and** help bring into existence things that none of them could easily make alone.

## How we work

**Practice, don't just prescribe.** Try to behave in ways consistent with the future you advocate.

**Remain distinct.** Working together does not require agreement on art, politics, philosophy, or what a perfect society would look like.

**Learn in public when possible.** Share what worked, what failed, what remains uncertain, and what you would try differently.

**Contribute without surrendering ownership.** Participation is opt-in. Artwork, credit, intellectual property, grant income, and responsibility stay with their rightful holders unless a specific agreement says otherwise.

**Make the collaboration itself worth joining.** The collective should help members create more, not merely ask them to serve a shared brand.

Our evolving [principles](PRINCIPLES.md) and [draft governance approach](GOVERNANCE.md) describe these commitments more fully.

## Where we begin

1. **Meet one another.** Artists and other collaborators introduce their work, the futures they are exploring, and what they might offer or need.
2. **Find an initial collaboration.** Propose an exhibition, a shared grant application, a mutual-promotion experiment, or another project small enough to attempt.
3. **Make it real.** Define participants, roles, credits, resources, a first action, and a way to tell whether it helped.
4. **Share and revise.** Let what happens inform the next attempt.

Some possible first projects: a group exhibition provisionally called *Utopia in the Present Tense*; a shared open-calls and grants watch; a network that promotes each member's practice; or a public event where artists demonstrate different ways of living a preferred future.

These are invitations, **not decisions already made by a collective**.

## Join or collaborate

You do not need to be a programmer to be part of FF Utopia. GitHub is a starting coordination space, not a membership test.

Read [How to participate](PARTICIPATE.md), or [propose a project](../../issues/new?template=project-idea.md). You can also open a general [issue](../../issues) to introduce a question or idea.

**This repository is a founding draft.** Its direction should be shaped with the people who join, rather than imposed on them in advance.
