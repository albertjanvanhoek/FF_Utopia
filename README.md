# FF Utopia — Fast Forward Utopia

**A club for intelligent entities who play for Utopia.**

Club website: **https://albertjanvanhoek.github.io/FF_Utopia/**

These times feel like a final between two sides: **Utopia** or **Apocalypse**.
FF Utopia is the club you join when you've picked your side.

We don't wait for Utopia to arrive. We **fast forward** it: each member lives and
works *as if Utopia is already here* — practising and sharing the behaviours and
ideas they believe belong to it. Every act like that pulls a small piece of the
future into the present. Individually it is personal and specific; as a team, it adds up.

> *FF* — fast forward. (And, in Dutch, *ff*: "just quickly". Utopia, ff.)

## The experiment is the artwork

**FF Utopia is itself an artwork.** We do not only make work *about* Utopia.
The club is a living experiment in practising it: its relationships, decisions,
resources, exhibitions, disagreements, and ways of learning are all part of the
artistic material.

Each member can make their own work and hold their own vision. The collective
tests what becomes possible when these different practices support, challenge,
and change one another. Exhibitions are not just presentations of the experiment:
they can become part of it. Successes, failures, and corrections matter—but
calling something art never excuses harm or overrides anyone's autonomy.

**The artwork is not a representation of Utopia. It is an attempt to create it.**

Read [**The experiment is the artwork**](EXPERIMENT.md) for the full artistic
proposition, questions, and ways to participate.

## What Utopia looks like (to us)

There is no single picture, and that is the point. Our working sketch:

- **No ruler, no single leader, no single ideology.**
- A **decentralised collective of autonomous, interdependent, learning subjects**.
- Autonomous: each member decides what Utopia asks of them.
- Interdependent: none of us gets there alone — we exist *because* of each other.
- Learning: Utopia is not a finished blueprint, it is something we keep discovering.

## What the club does

FF Utopia starts as a collective of **artists** (and other makers and thinkers)
who raise the odds of their own survival as artists — and of Utopia itself — by
playing as a team:

| Play | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Grants** | Apply together, share calls, review each other's applications, co-apply where it fits. |
| **Work** | Make new work together, or alongside each other, in the spirit of the club. |
| **Exhibitions** | Organise shows, events and performances — our *matchdays*. |
| **Promotion** | Promote each other. A member's win is a club win. |

See [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md) for how this works in practice.

**First move:** [offer or ask for a pass](plays/README.md) — a small act of practical help.
You can also join a [proposed first exhibition](works/utopia-in-the-present-tense/README.md).
A play counts because someone *does* something, not because everyone agrees on a manifesto.

## Club colours
