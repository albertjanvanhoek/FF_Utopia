Play for Utopia. Starting now.

These times feel like a final between two sides. FF Utopia is the club you join once you've picked yours.

We don't wait for Utopia to arrive. Each member lives and works as if it is already here — practising and sharing the behaviours and ideas they believe belong to it. Every act like that pulls a small piece of the future into the present. Alone it is personal. As a team, it adds up.

What Utopia looks like

There is no single picture, and that is the point. No ruler, no single leader, no single ideology. Our working sketch is a decentralised collective of subjects who are:

  • AutonomousEach member decides what Utopia asks of them.
  • InterdependentNobody gets there alone. We exist because of each other.
  • LearningUtopia isn't a finished blueprint. It's something we keep discovering.

How we play

The club starts with artists, makers and thinkers who raise the odds of their own survival — and of Utopia's — by playing as a team.

Grants
Share open calls, review each other's applications, and apply together where it fits. Grant calendar
Work
Make new work together or alongside each other, with credit always shared openly.
Matchdays
Exhibitions, concerts, readings, screenings, open studios. Every public moment of the club ends with the victory hum.
Promotion
The pass rule: when you promote your own work, look for a chance to promote another member's work you genuinely appreciate. An invitation, not a quota. Club news
No captain
Anyone can start something. Club-wide decisions pass unless someone raises a reasoned objection. The playbook

Join the squad

Open to any intelligent entity — human artists, makers, scientists, thinkers, collectives and AIs. No fee, no exam. Three commitments:

  1. Play forward. Practise at least one behaviour or idea you believe belongs to Utopia, and share it.
  2. Play for each other. Share an opportunity, review an application, promote a work, show up at a matchday.
  3. Play without a captain. Respect every member's autonomy. Nobody's Utopia is the only Utopia.

Add your card to the repository with a pull request — or ask a member to add it for you.
